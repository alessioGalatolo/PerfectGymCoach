## Elevate Fitness: Privacy policy

Welcome to the Elevate Fitness app for Android!

This is an open-source Android app developed by Alessio Galatolo. The source code is available on GitHub under the GPL v3 license.

### Data collected by the app

All data (app preferences, name, age, workout history, etc.) is stored locally on your device only, and can be erased by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected or transmitted.

### Crash reports

The phone app uses **Firebase Crashlytics** (provided by Google) for crash reporting. Crash reporting is on by default and can be turned off at any time in *Profile → Preferences → Send crash reports*.

- **What is sent:** only when the app crashes, a crash report containing technical information: device model, OS version, available memory and disk space, app version, and the stack trace of the crash. Each report is linked to a random installation identifier generated by Firebase. This identifier is not tied to your name, account or advertising ID, but it is a pseudonymous identifier, so crash reports are not fully anonymous. Your IP address is necessarily visible to Google when the report is uploaded. Crash reports never include your workout data, profile data or any health information.
- **Why:** to find and fix bugs that make the app crash. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in keeping the app working (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object at any time by turning the setting off.
- **Who processes it:** Google acts as data processor on behalf of the developer, under the [Firebase data processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms). Data may be processed outside the EU; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- **How long it is kept:** crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. See [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) for details.

The app does not use any analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not access the device's advertising ID.

### Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Since all other data stays on your device, these rights mainly concern crash reports: you can stop them by turning off *Send crash reports*, and you can ask for existing reports linked to your installation to be deleted by contacting the developer (see below).

### Explanation of permissions requested in the app

**Access media notification**
This permission is used to access music playing on device (if any) so that it can also be controlled from within the app. This permission still requires manual user confirmation.

**Post notifications**
The app requests permission to send notifications in order to display an ongoing workout indicator while a session is active, and to show a live countdown of your remaining rest time between sets. No notification data is collected or transmitted.

**Background Processing**
A foreground service is used to keep your workout session running reliably while the app is in the background, ensuring your workout data and rest timer remain accurate even when the screen is off or you switch to another app.

**Wear OS Companion**
The Wear OS companion app uses the same notification and background permissions as above. In addition, it requests:
- **Vibration** – to alert you with a haptic buzz when your rest period ends.
- **Exact Alarms** – to schedule the rest timer with precise timing, so the vibration and notification trigger at the right moment.
- **Heart Rate/Body Sensors** – to track your heart rate during the workout and report it as metric.
- **Exercise Activity** – to track more accurately the calories you burn during the workout.

**Health Connect**
The companion phone app integrates with Health Connect to read and write your health data. The following permissions are requested:
- **Write Exercise** – to save your completed workout sessions to Health Connect.
- **Write Active Calories Burned** – to record the active calories burned during a workout session.

All health data shared with Health Connect stays on your device and is governed by your Health Connect privacy settings. No health data is transmitted to external servers by this app.

---

All of the above features function entirely on your device. No data is sent to external servers as a result of these permissions. The only data that ever leaves your device is the crash reports described above.
